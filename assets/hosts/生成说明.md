# SceneFlow 内置人物素材

工作台随项目和 Portable 包提供两段可直接选择的循环视频：

- `sceneflow-host-female-loop-v1.mp4`：新项目默认人物形象。
- `sceneflow-host-male-loop-v1.mp4`：内置男主持备选。

静态参考图使用内置 imagegen 技能/工具生成，未使用 CLI 或本地图片生成模型。

工作台同时保留视频生成用的静态参考图 `solo-host-v1-1080.jpg`（1920×1080，居中适配画幅）。原始生成文件另保留在 Codex 的 generated_images 中。

最终生成提示词：

One fictional Chinese man in his early 30s, warm intelligent approachable face, clean-shaven, short neat black hair, wearing a simple charcoal knit crewneck. Seated at a wooden podcast desk in a tastefully furnished quiet recording studio with softly blurred bookshelves, understated warm practical lighting, acoustic wall panels. Medium close shot from mid chest upward; face centered and fairly large (face at least 320 pixels tall in a 1080-high frame), looking directly at camera, head upright front-facing, calm neutral expression with gently closed relaxed lips. A professional black podcast microphone on a boom arm sits low to the side, clearly below the chin and NEVER covering lips, jaw or cheeks. Facial landmarks, mouth edges, chin and both cheeks completely visible. Even soft natural key lighting on entire face, realistic detailed skin, premium documentary photography, restrained background, sharp eyes and face. Some breathing room above head, shoulders visible, no hands near face, no second person, no glasses, no beard, no text, no logos, no captions, no watermark. Intended as a MuseTalk lip-sync source image: unobstructed frontal face and neutral closed mouth are essential.
